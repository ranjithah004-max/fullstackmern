1. Lion

    The lion is a large wild animal known as the king of the jungle. Lions live mainly in grasslands and are known for their strength and social behavior.

  2. Elephant

    The elephant is the largest land animal on Earth. It has a long trunk, large ears, and strong legs. Elephants are intelligent and social animals.

  3. Tiger

    The tiger is a powerful wild cat recognized by its orange coat and black stripes. Tigers are excellent hunters and usually live alone.

  4. Giraffe

    The giraffe is the tallest land animal in the world. It has a very long neck and legs, which help it reach leaves high in trees.
